## 🚀 About Me

Hey, I'm **Nishant** 👋 — a QA professional with **6.5+ years** of breaking things (professionally) who's now leveling up to **build and automate them** as a DevOps Engineer.

- 🐞 Started my career hunting bugs as a **Manual QA Engineer** — now hunting inefficiencies in pipelines and infra.
- 🔥 On a mission to transition into **DevOps**, training hard on Shell, Docker, Kubernetes, Jenkins, GitHub Actions, Terraform, Ansible, Prometheus, Grafana & Helm.
- 🎯 Aiming for roles in **Platform Engineering, SRE, DevOps Engineering, and SDET** — where "quality mindset meets automation" is my unfair advantage.
- 🐍 Sharpening my **Shell & Python** skills for scripting and automation.
- 💬 Ask me about **Testing, CI/CD, and my QA-to-DevOps journey**.
- ⚡ Fun fact: I still write test cases in my head for everything I automate.
